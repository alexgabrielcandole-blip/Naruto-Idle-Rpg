57. Priority 5A: mobile navigation, information access and
interrupted-session integrity
2 November 2026 | Version 0.10.30 | RESEARCH/CONCEPT ONLY. This packet is a logical UI and state-
ownership architecture, NOT a designed screen mockup, clickable prototype, backend, telemetry deployment,
or observed usability finding. Existing §§24–27 combat and §§40, 52–56 economy/campaign rules remain
authoritative.

57.1 Problem, design principles and source boundaries
[QUESTION] On a compact phone, can a first-time or returning player always answer four questions—What
should I do next? What will it cost? Why did I lose? Did my reward actually post?—without memorizing a
large menu tree or risking duplicate grants? The answer must preserve the existing tactical and financial
contracts.

[NON-NEGOTIABLE DEPENDENCIES] Starter entitlements START00 and explicit START01; training grants
ONB01–04, aggregate band upgrade and Hall20/Band-I state; E0 idle accrual at ONB01; tutorial FIRST01; G1–
G8/SCN graph; chapter FIRST02; G8 FIRST03 (80 Ryo/120 EXP); STORY01/02/03 grant before their gates;
optional TA-01; 34 seals/60 fragments per full 28-day core; existing banner/selector disclosure; no new
currencies or payout events. UI projection cannot edit these source rules.

[EVIDENCE] Official Android UX recommends 3–5 top-level destinations on compact screens, ≥48dp touch
targets, and adaptive navigation for larger form factors. W3C WCAG 2.2 establishes a distinct web target-size
minimum (24 CSS pixels subject to exceptions)—not a substitute for Android phone touch guidance. Android
official state-restoration documentation distinguishes transient UI state from durable application data. All
exact click paths and screen labels below are our own proposals, not externally validated benchmarks. See
§57.11.

[TRUST BOUNDARY UX-02] Never derive owned currency or first-clear rewards from an animation, local
countdown or toast. The UI may show provisional estimates, but only an authoritative transaction receipt
changes the displayed confirmed wallet. Receipts expose source ID, debit/credit, before/after balance when
available, and whether already claimed. Back, retry and reconnect must not replay the grant.

[INFORMATION SEPARATION] Display user-earned CORE01/02 objectively; FIRST01/02/03 as one-time;
STORY01/02/03 as noncurrency first copies; TA-01 emblem once; E0 as time-accrual. Do not combine these into
a misleading “daily prize” total. A locked mode is labeled “Not unlocked” with its actual condition, not
“Claimable.”

57.9 Decision register, trade-offs, falsifiers and exclusions
[SELECTED PROPOSALS] UX-01 four persistent roots; contextual Hall/Idle/Archive/Dossier; battle-focused
overlays; one dominant actionable next step; priority P0–P5; explicit transactional receipts;
preflight→battle→recap with causally grounded explanations; two presentation paths with identical
progression. These are design decisions for future testing, not coded features.

[REJECTED] A fifth empty Modes tab; forced separate UI modal per SCN node; automatic START01 timeout
choice; Home as a monetization billboard; treating a wallet animation as credit authority; claiming every
defeat means insufficient power; assigning imaginary eligible sources; adding a new currency to make a
menu feel busy.

[COSTS / TRADE-OFFS] Four roots push future social/endgame modes into a later navigation review; Home
contextual shortcuts increase conditional UI complexity; exposing receipts requires a deliberate data schema;
preflight explanations consume limited screen space; safely hiding commercial prompts may reduce early
monetization impressions. These costs are accepted provisionally to preserve player agency, fairness and
trust.

[FALSIFIERS / REOPEN IF] An authorized small-device study shows a substantial share of players cannot find
Recruitment/Hall/Archive; first-session players misinterpret the support choice; returners mistake capped
accrual for lost rewards; effect icons remain unreadable at enlarged text; preflight cannot show legal counters
without deep nested navigation; critical actions exceed touch target bounds; root nav changes break
uninterrupted battles. No observed data exists yet.

57.10 Next research handoff and performance-level choice
[NEXT RESEARCH — PRIORITY 5B] Specify screen-by-screen minimum content and hierarchy on a small
portrait phone: Home, Squad/Hall, Campaign/Preflight, Battle/Recap, Recruit/Wallet and claim flows. Write the
exact short player-facing copy for FIRST01/02/03, START01 and idle overflow without changing transaction
amounts. Test static paper layouts against 48dp target envelopes and large text, with explicit missing-space
notes. Then trace a first session and returning session, comparing whether players can reach the next
meaningful action and understand why. Performance recommendation: HIGH for bounded visual
hierarchy/copy once §57 route architecture is stable; use EXTRA HIGH if downstream state, purchase or
combat semantics must be reconciled. No production, prototype, simulator or live user test until owner
authorizes.

58.1 Decision scope and invariant checklist
[DESIGN QUESTION] What is the smallest amount of truthful information each critical phone screen can show
before the player acts, while still exposing a tactical response, real source/cost, and confirmed reward state?
The current objective is legibility and state honesty, not maximizing the number of metrics on screen.

[UNCHANGED CONTRACT] Four roots: Home, Squad, Campaign and Recruit. Child pages: Hall, Idle, Preflight,
Battle, Recap, Wallet, Details and Tactical Archive. START00 four recruits; ONB01–04 to Hall20/Band I;
START01 explicit K-09 or K-10; FIRST01 five seals; FIRST02 five seals; FIRST03 80 Ryo and 120 EXP;
STORY01/02/03 K-08/K-04/K-17; G1–G8; TA-01 cosmetic emblem. Current CORE01/02 and E0 are unchanged.
Pity and acquisition visibility remains controlled by §§36–40.

[INFORMATION PRIORITY] First: an unresolved integrity state or irreversible choice; second: next legal action
with cost/eligibility; third: immediate tactical reason; fourth: secondary details. Never use hidden tooltips as
the sole carrier of a dangerous enemy intent, source shortfall or irreversible selection.

59.6 Edge-case walkthroughs and recoverability proofs still owed

60.1 Decision boundary, evidence and non-regression rules
[PROJECT CONSTRAINTS] Preserve the four-root navigation (Home/Squad/Campaign/Recruit), Hall20/Band-I

60.2 Localization policy: one source language, expandable content architecture
[WORKING DECISION 5D-01] Author initial text in English. Treat Filipino, Japanese, Burmese, and right-to-left
languages as research candidates, NOT committed launch languages. The first actual supported-language list
must follow audience, translation-cost, font/shaping and QA research; no locale is “supported” until its
complete gameplay, economy, legal and recovery strings have been reviewed. Store all UI text, status names,
dialog, tooltips and narrative copy as stable localization keys, not text burned into art. Select a documented
fallback locale and log missing keys.

[DESIGN RULE] Do not concatenate translated fragments to construct text such as “You got” + quantity + noun.
Use locale-aware placeholders and plural variants, localized number/decimal and currency display when
applicable, explicit language metadata, and line wraps that tolerate expansion. Preserve stable mechanical IDs
(G1, K-09, FIRST03) internally; localize readable names and descriptions. Distinguish virtual Ryo from actual
paid currency. The game world uses Ryo as a resource name, not as a promise of real-world yen conversion.

[RTL POLICY] Future right-to-left support requires direction-aware navigation, focus and bidirectional text
handling. Never mechanically mirror battle formation slot identities, directional attack indicators or target
logic merely because the interface is mirrored; preserve underlying combat coordinates and make visual
reading order explicit. Use logical start/end alignment in presentation concepts. This is a planned
compatibility gate, not a launch feature commitment.

60.4 Connectivity contract: readable cached state is not offline progression
[WORKING DECISION 5D-03] Allow cached read-only access to the latest *confirmed* Home/Squad/Campaign
information, previously saved story and past recaps when offline. Display a visible “Last confirmed”

settlement and other authoritative writes require confirmation by the chosen future service, or must remain
visibly pending with immutable request identity.

60.7 Decisions, trade-offs, remaining blockers and next handoff
[SELECTED] English-authoring with localization-ready IDs, optional future locales; 30fps effects-reduced
candidate (not measured), cache-confirmed read-only offline shell, authoritative writes, reduced-motion
equivalence; mobile UX requirements are paper-defined only. [REJECTED] simultaneous unreviewed
multilingual launch, cosmetic settings changing combat mechanics, client-computed spendable rewards,
mandatory fully-online access to previously confirmed information. [DEPENDENCIES] Section 59 receipt
integrity, Section 57 navigation, Sections 24–27 combat event logs, Section 40 source IDs, Section 52 tutorial
and Section 56 story records.

[OPEN] Supported languages and typography by market; exact device tier, minimum RAM/storage,
download/data budgets and connection assumptions; keyboard/screen-reader integration in the eventual
engine; how guest/cross-device identity works; production backend and security model; measured
accessibility, thermal, memory and rendering criteria; actual legal/licensing plan. None of these is claimed
solved.

[NEXT RESEARCH — PRIORITY 6A: COMPETITIVE/SOLO ACCESS AND NORMALIZED ARENA ARCHITECTURE]
Map whether social/PvP participation is optional, how matchmaking and normalized roster investments avoid
paying for mandatory counters, how progression-account and competitive reward sources separate, and what
a solo player retains. Recommend EXTRA HIGH because this joins PvP combat, acquisition, entitlement and
reward economy; avoid writing actual matchmaking code or reporting win rates. A later 6B pass will address
guild/co-op and solo-equivalent pathways.
