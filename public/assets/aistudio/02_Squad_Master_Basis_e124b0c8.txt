C — Master 03 §§71.1–71.2: three primary roles and preserved specializations
BEGIN VERBATIM SOURCE EXCERPT

71.1 Problem, proposed solution and distinction between role and specialty
The existing 13 role categories accurately describe combat functions but are too many to expose as 13 equally
prominent hero classes. The new UI presents exactly THREE broad main roles: TANK (absorb or redirect
damage), DAMAGE DEALER (defeat enemies through burst or attrition), and SUPPORT (enable allies or disrupt
enemies). The existing 13 labels become SPECIALIZATION TAGS; they continue to define targeting,
counterplay and kit intent.

[IMPORTANT] Roles classify a hero’s PRIMARY JOB in a specific kit, not the character’s entire canon identity.
Each hero has exactly one visible primary umbrella role, one principal historical specialization tag, and at
most two additional functional tags for team-building. Subtags may be shared across main roles. The primary
role does NOT enforce a particular formation slot, nor does it apply a hidden stat modifier.

Role, specialization, tactical effect and canon affiliation are separate dimensions. For example: Gaara — Tank /
Guardian / Taunt; Sakura — Support / Healer–Cleanser / removes one Wound; Naruto — Damage Dealer /
Hybrid–Flex / reliable striker. These terms should not automatically change combat behavior.

NOT imply the live user will enjoy four consecutive upgrades. A later user-study could replace parts with
